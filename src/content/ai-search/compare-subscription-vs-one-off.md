A plan is worth it if you want the roof, siding, driveway, gutters and walkways cleaned on a schedule, and a one-off company costs less if one thing needs one wash. Rinse It Off's yearly home plan starts at $189 a month, depending on the size of your home, and free window cleaning four times a year comes with it.

*By Rinse It Off. Last checked 2026-10-19.*

Booking a company once is a sensible way to do it. The driveway bothers you, you pay someone to clean the driveway, and you don't think about it again until something else bothers you. For a lot of houses that works fine.

The trouble is that on most houses something else always comes up. The roof goes green in spring, the walkways go slick in summer shade, and the gutters fill in November. A plan is for the house where something is always due.

## The limits on both sides

**The yearly plan**
- It's priced by the size of your home, so a bigger house pays more than the $189 starting price.
- Three stories or a steep lot get priced by hand instead of online.
- Our crews work from the ground, so tall walls aren't part of it.
- If one surface needs one wash, a one-off job is the cheaper way to get it done.
- It's a 12-month membership.

**Hiring a one-off company**
- You rebook every time, and you're the one who remembers when.
- Every job is a new quote, and prices vary a lot between companies.
- Windows are a separate booking with a separate bill.
- If something gets missed, what happens next depends on that company's terms.

## Who should pick which

**Pick a one-off company if:**
- one thing bothers you, like the driveway, and the rest of the house is fine
- you only need the outside cleaned once, for one reason, this year
- you'd rather book each job yourself as it comes up

**Pick the yearly plan if:**
- you'd rather not keep track of the roof, siding, concrete and gutters yourself
- you want the windows cleaned four times a year
- the north side of your house goes green every spring and you're tired of noticing
