## What moves the price

| What | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Stories | A second story takes longer to reach and longer to rinse. |
| Square footage | More wall means more cleaning solution and more time. |
| Siding | Vinyl, cedar, stucco and painted wood each need a different touch. |
| How much is growing | Light dust rinses off. Thick green on the north side takes more work. |
| Access | Steep yards, tight side yards and shrubs against the wall slow things down. |
| Add-ons | Gutters, the driveway and the windows each add their own line. |

## Soft wash or pressure wash on a house

Most houses here get soft washed, not pressure washed. A soft wash puts a cleaning solution on the siding at low pressure, lets it do the work, then rinses it off gently. We don't pressure wash siding, because high pressure drives water behind the boards and strips paint.

Pressure still has a job. We use it on driveways and walkways, because concrete can take it. If a quote says "pressure wash" and the house has siding, ask which method goes on the walls. More on that in [soft wash vs pressure wash](/blog/soft-wash-vs-pressure-wash).

## How to get a clean quote from any company

1. Know how many stories the house has and what the siding is made of.
2. Ask for the quote in writing, with each surface listed on its own line.
3. Ask which method they'll use on the siding. On most siding, the answer you want is a soft wash.
4. Ask what insurance they carry, and ask for the certificate.
5. Ask what happens if a spot gets missed. With us, you tell us and we re-rinse it free within 48 hours.

## Questions people ask

**What is the average price to soft wash a two-story house in the Portland area?**
About $350 to $500, by one Portland-area price table. Another guide prices by size instead, and lists $400 to $600 for a house of 1,500 to 2,500 square feet.

**Does soft washing cost more than pressure washing?**
The guides don't split them cleanly. HomeAdvisor lists $250 to $1,000 to soft wash most homes and $213 to $449 for a typical power wash, but the soft wash range covers larger houses too. Ask for the method on the written quote and compare like with like.

**Does a house wash include the windows?**
Ask, because a house wash and a window cleaning are different jobs. We clean windows with pure water, which is water with the minerals filtered out, so it dries without spots. On our yearly home plan, window cleaning four times a year is free.

**What is a fair price for house washing and gutter cleaning together?**
From the same Portland-area table: a one-story house wash at $250 to $350 plus gutters at $150 to $200 comes to $400 to $550. For two stories, $350 to $500 plus $200 to $300 comes to $550 to $800.
