## Does moss actually damage a roof?

Moss can. The Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association says moss can lift or curl the edges of shingles, which raises the risk of them blowing off. In bad cases, it says, moss can let moisture into the roof deck and cause leaks. Oregon State University Extension says much the same: moss holds moisture and debris against the roof and can lift shingles so water gets underneath.

The black streaks are a different story. ARMA says those streaks are algae, and it hasn't been shown to damage shingles. It mostly affects how the roof looks. That's worth knowing before anyone sells you an emergency.

If you treat it yourself, these are the sources' methods, not ours. ARMA's method is laundry-strength chlorine bleach mixed 50:50 with water, left on for 15 to 20 minutes, then rinsed with low-pressure water. Oregon State University Extension lists zinc sulfate monohydrate at 3 pounds in 9 gallons of water, which covers 600 to 1,000 square feet, and products made from potassium salts of fatty acids.

## How to deal with moss on your roof

1. Look from the ground first. Check the north slope and anything under trees.
2. Trim back the branches so sun reaches the roof. OSU calls this the main long-term fix. Clear off the debris too.
3. Pick a dry stretch.
4. Treat it with one of the methods above, or hire it out.
5. Rinse at low pressure. ARMA's method ends that way.
6. Give it a few weeks. Moss lets go slowly once it's dead.
7. Keep the gutters clear, because the moss coming off the roof ends up in them.
8. Think about prevention carefully. ARMA doesn't recommend adding zinc or copper strips to an existing roof, and OSU says zinc corrodes copper gutters and flashing.

If you're doing steps 4 and 5 yourself, OSU says the ladder should reach 3 feet above the roofline.

## Questions people ask

**How much does roof moss removal cost in Portland?**
Portland-area price guides put cleaning a composition roof at about $300 to $600. Steep roofs, three stories and heavy growth cost more. On our yearly home plan, a spring roof moss treatment and soft wash are included.

**How do you get moss off a roof without damaging the shingles?**
Kill it with a treatment at low pressure and let it loosen, rather than blasting it off. ARMA's own method ends with a low-pressure rinse. We soft wash roofs with a cleaning solution at low pressure, then rinse gently.

**Is it bad to pressure wash a roof?**
On asphalt shingles, yes. High pressure knocks off the granules that protect the roof, and it can push water under the shingles. Oregon State University Extension says experienced users sometimes do it, but poor technique can damage shingles.

**Does moss on my roof actually damage it?**
It can. ARMA says moss can lift or curl shingle edges and, in bad cases, let moisture into the roof deck. Black algae streaks are different: ARMA says they haven't been shown to damage shingles.

**Should I remove moss before replacing my roof?**
Ask the roofer doing the replacement, because it's their call on their job. The guidance we've cited here is about moss on a roof you're keeping.

**Will roof cleaning void my shingle warranty?**
Check your shingle maker's warranty before anyone cleans the roof. ARMA, the trade group for asphalt shingle makers, recommends cleaning with a treatment and a low-pressure rinse.
