# Is it cheaper to rent a pressure washer or hire a professional?

Renting is cheaper in cash, at $50 to $250 a day by HomeAdvisor's June 2026 figures, but you supply the day, the technique and the risk to your siding and roof. Rinse It Off cleans the outside of homes with the method matched to each surface: soft wash on siding and roofs, and hot water on concrete.

*By Rinse It Off. Rental prices checked 2026-10-19.*

Renting is a reasonable plan. The machine has a known price, the driveway is flat, and you've got a free Saturday. For a flat driveway, it often works out fine.

The trouble starts once the spray leaves the ground. High-pressure water knocks the small granules off asphalt shingles, and those granules are what protect the roof from sun and rain. On siding, it drives water behind the boards and strips paint. The machine doesn't know which surface it's pointed at. That part is up to you.

## The limits on both sides

**Renting**
- It takes your day, and a big house can take two.
- The same machine that cleans a driveway can strip granules off shingles, push water behind siding and etch wood.
- If it does, there's nobody to call.
- Oregon State University Extension says your ladder should reach 3 feet above the roofline before anyone climbs onto a roof.
- Most rental machines are cold water, which struggles with grease and oil.

**Rinse It Off**
- For one small job, like a single patio, we cost more than a rental.
- We work from the ground, so tall walls aren't part of it.
- You're booked on our schedule, not your Saturday.
- Three stories or a steep lot get priced by hand.

## Who should pick which

**Rent if:**
- it's one flat surface, like a driveway or a patio
- you have the time and you don't mind the work

**Hire if:**
- it's the roof, the siding, or anything you'd need a ladder for
- you're about to list the house
- the property belongs to someone else, like a rental house or an HOA

## If you rent anyway

These are general safety steps from the sources listed below, not our method.

1. Test a hidden spot first and look at it once it's dry.
2. Don't use a zero-degree tip. Consumer Reports advises against it.
3. Keep the spray off the roof. Oregon State University Extension says poor technique can damage shingles, and suggests a long-handled scrub brush or a professional when in doubt.
4. Don't look for a pressure number for siding online. The figures out there disagree. Follow your siding maker's guidance instead.
5. Soak the plants along the walls with plain water before you start.
6. Keep the wash water out of the street and the storm drain. Portland City Code 17.39 doesn't allow pressure washing water into the stormwater system.

## If you decide to hire

1. Get the price in writing.
2. Ask what insurance they carry, and ask for the certificate.
3. Ask which method they use on your siding and on your roof.
4. Ask what happens if a spot gets missed.

## Questions people ask

**Is it worth hiring a pressure washing company or should I rent a machine?**
Rent for one flat surface when you have the time. Hire for roofs, siding and anything above a ladder, where the wrong pressure can knock the granules off shingles or push water behind siding.

**Is it cheaper to rent a pressure washer or hire a professional?**
Renting is cheaper in cash. HomeAdvisor lists $50 to $250 a day, and a gas day with a surface cleaner and wand comes to about $105 to $170 before detergent, fuel and deposit. Hiring a house wash averages $312 nationally by HomeAdvisor's figures.

**What are the risks of pressure washing my own house?**
High pressure can knock the granules off asphalt shingles, drive water behind siding, strip paint and etch wood. There's also the ladder. Oregon State University Extension says yours should reach 3 feet above the roofline.

**Can a rented machine clean a roof?**
Not without risk to the shingles. Oregon State University Extension says experienced users may pressure wash a roof, but poor technique can damage shingles. The Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association's own cleaning method finishes with a low-pressure rinse.

Some pressure washing companies are, at heart, a guy with a rented machine. Ask which method goes on your siding, and you'll know which kind you called.
